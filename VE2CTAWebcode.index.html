1 Making Suggestions (提出建議)

VE2C Page 2-3
🐶 Pattern A: Using 'suggest'
  • 1. suggest + Noun Phrase:
    "I suggest a charity concert."
  • 2. suggest + Gerund (v-ing):
    "I suggest organising a charity concert."
  • 3. suggest + that-clause:
    "I suggest (that) we (should) organise a charity concert."
🐱 Pattern B: Using Modals
  • I think we should + Base Verb:
    "I think we should organise a charity concert."
  • Perhaps/Maybe we can/could + Base Verb:
    "Perhaps we could organise a charity concert."
  • It might be a good idea / advisable to + verb:
    "It might be a good idea to organise a charity concert."
🐾 Pattern C: Introducing a Topic

Topic Introducers:

Regarding Concerning About For / As for

"Regarding the venue, I suggest the Assembly Hall."

"Concerning the walkathon, I suggest Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade."

2 Giving Explanations & Reasons (給予解釋及原因)

VE2C Page 4-5

🐶 Connectors: because / since / as

These conjunctions are followed by a complete clause (Subject + Verb).

"It might be a good idea to schedule the show for 26 December evening since it is a public holiday."

"I suggest Yuen Long Community Centre because the location is convenient."

🐱 6 Simple Sentence Patterns for Reasons

  • 1. It is + Prepositional Phrase: "It is in Central."
  • 2. The location is + Adjective: "The location is convenient."
  • 3. It can + Base Verb: "It can attract more people."
  • 4. It has/have + Object: "It has a lighting system."
  • 5. It costs + Price: "It costs only $100."
  • 6. There is/are + Subject: "There are 300 seats."

🐾 Giving Multiple Reasons (多重原因結構)

Reasoning Intro Phrase:

"Regarding the location, I suggest conducting the show at JCCAC for some reasons." / "...because of the following."

1st Reason "First, it is in Shek Kip Mei."
2nd Reason "Also, it accommodates 300 people."
3rd Reason "Moreover, the rental is affordable."

3 Stating Preferences (表達偏好)

VE2C Page 6

🐶 Expressing Enjoyment (prefer ... to ...)

Formula 1:

I prefer [Option A] to [Option B]

e.g., "I prefer an art exhibition to a drawing competition."

Formula 2 (with Gerunds):

I prefer [Verb-ing A] to [Verb-ing B]

e.g., "I prefer organising the Tree Planting Day to visiting the Organic Farm."

🐱 Expressing Choices (would prefer to ...)

Formula:

I would prefer to + Base Verb [Option A]

e.g., "I would prefer to join the STEM Workshop."

Alternative with 'like':

I like [Option A] better than [Option B]

e.g., "I like sports days better than singing contests."

4 Describing Community Activities (描述社區活動)

VE2C Page 7-8
🎯 1. Describing Activity Aim
  • "The aim of the activity is to promote healthy living."
  • "It aims to reduce food waste."
  • "We hope to promote recycling through the activity."
👥 2. Target Participants
  • "The target participants are school children."
  • "It is suitable for all local residents."
  • "We welcome members of our centre."
📌 3. Activity Content
  • "The activity will include basic gardening workshops."
  • "The participants will join a visit to Tai Po Fishing Village."

5 Giving Elaborations (詳細說明與補充)

VE2C Page 9-10

🐶 1. Using Adjectives

Describe the nature of the activity before a noun phrase:

"It is an educational activity."
"This meaningful walkathon aims to raise funds."

🐱 2. Using Relative Clauses

Provide details about participants using who:

"...suitable for students who are interested in science."
"...suitable for residents who are keen on outdoor sports."

🐾 3. Using First Conditional

Elaborate content (If + Present Simple, will + Verb):

"If colleagues join this activity, they will receive a souvenir."

6 Making Offers (主動提供協助)

VE2C Page 11

🐶 Modal Verbs for Offers (Followed by Base Form Verb)

Shall I ...?

"Shall I create a post on social media to reach a wider audience?"

Would you like me to ...?

"Would you like me to contact group members to collect their ideas?"

I could ...

"I could email the venue manager to reserve and confirm the venue."

7 Common Expressions in Email Replies (回覆電郵常用語)

VE2C Page 12-13

✉️ 1. Opening & Acknowledging

  • "Dear Chris / Dear Mr Wong,"
  • "Thank you very much for your email concerning the charity events."
  • "In response to your email, I would like to state my preference."

🎯 2. Purpose of Writing

  • "I am writing to state my preference and make suggestions."
  • "I am writing to introduce the details of our upcoming walkathon."

👋 3. Closing Expressions

  • "I hope my ideas are useful."
  • "I look forward to discussing these ideas further with you."
  • "Please feel free to contact me if you need more information."
